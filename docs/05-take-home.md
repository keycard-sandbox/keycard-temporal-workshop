# Optional take-home exercises

Use the instructor-supplied Instruqt track when available. Confirm its availability, access end time, and whether work is retained with the instructor; a downloaded package does not guarantee continued shared-service access.

- Create two matching expenses, reload the page to clear selection, and ask to approve by description. Resolve the Expense Desk agent's clarification with an exact ID.
- Compare $42 travel, $75 supplies, and $125 equipment; ask a partner to approve each and compare the results with their approval limit.
- Explore an activity whose external payment commits but whose response is lost. Design an API-side idempotency key or reconciliation step before retrying payment.

Use fresh expenses and an isolated environment. Provisioning instructions are in the instructor handoff; these investigations are outside the 60-minute session.
